Privacy policy

What we collect, why we collect it, and the choices you have.

Last updated November 5, 2026

Experiment Labs (“we”, “us”) makes Experiment AI. This policy explains what we collect when you use experimentai.dev and the Experiment AI app (together, the “service”), how we use it, who helps us run the service, and the choices you have.

The short version: we collect only what we need to run Experiment AI, we never sell your personal information, we don’t train AI models on your content, and we don’t use cookies.

1. What we collect

  • Account details. Your name and email address. If you sign up with email, your password is handled by Firebase Authentication; we never see it. If you sign in with Google, we receive the name, email address and profile photo on your Google account. If you upload your own profile picture, we keep it with your account.
  • Email verification. When you sign up with email, we send you a 6-digit code. We store only a scrambled (hashed) version of it, with when it was sent and how many attempts were made, to stop abuse.
  • Your chats and projects. The messages you send, the answers you get back, your projects and your settings are saved to your account so you can use them on any device. Your browser also keeps a copy so the app opens quickly; it’s cleared when you log out.
  • Files and media. Files you attach (such as pictures, PDFs and text files) and the images and videos Experiment AI makes for you are stored in Vercel Blob, our file storage. Each file gets a long, random link. Anyone who has a link can open that file, so only share links you’re happy to share.
  • Voice. When you use dictation, your recording is sent to a speech-to-text model to turn it into text. We don’t keep the recording.
  • Memory. On paid plans, with Memory on in a chat, Experiment AI can save short facts you share (for example, how you like answers) and use them in later chats. You can see, add, delete or clear them in Settings › Personalization. Chats with Memory switched off don’t read your Memory and don’t add to it.
  • Connected services. Only if you connect them: Google Drive (only the files you pick), Notion (only the pages you share), GitHub (only the repositories where you install our app) and Canva. We store the access each service gives us on our servers, never in your browser, and you can disconnect at any time.
  • Usage. How much of your plan you’ve used today and this week, and which models you used, so we can show your Usage and apply your plan’s limits.
  • Payments. Stripe handles payments. We keep your plan, whether it’s monthly or yearly, its status and renewal date, and the Stripe customer ID that links them. We never see or store your full card number.
  • Account status. If an account is suspended for breaking our Terms, we keep a record of when and why.
  • Site statistics and logs. We use Google Analytics in a cookie-free mode to count page views. Our host, Vercel, keeps standard server logs (such as IP address, browser type and time of request) for security and reliability.
  • Messages to us. If you email contact@experimentai.dev, we keep that conversation.

2. How we use it

  • To create your account, confirm your email and keep you signed in.
  • To run Experiment AI: answer your messages, read the files you attach, make images and videos, search the web when it helps, remember things when Memory is on, and use the services you connect.
  • To apply your plan, its usage limits and its payments.
  • To email you about your account and verification codes. We don’t send unrelated marketing.
  • To keep the service secure, prevent abuse, enforce our Terms and fix problems.
  • To understand which pages people use, so we can improve the site.
  • To follow the law.

We don’t sell your personal information, and we don’t use it to show you ads.

3. AI models and your content

Experiment AI gives you models from several companies, including Anthropic (Claude), OpenAI (GPT), Google (Gemini and Veo), xAI (Grok) and Meta (Muse). When you send a message, your message, the files you attach and the relevant part of the conversation go to the model you chose, through Vercel’s AI Gateway, so it can answer. Each company processes that content under its own terms for business use of its models.

  • We don’t train AI models on your content.
  • Web search: when a question needs current information (like tonight’s game or today’s news), Experiment AI searches the web on its own using Brave Search. Only the search words are sent, not your name or account.
  • Connected services: when you switch a connected service on for a chat, the AI can read from it (and, for Canva, create designs) to answer you.

4. Google user data

If you connect Google Drive, Experiment AI’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. In particular:

  • We only use Google data to provide the features you ask for, such as reading a file you picked.
  • We don’t use Google data for advertising, and we don’t sell it.
  • We don’t use Google Workspace data to develop, improve or train generalized AI or machine learning models.
  • No person at Experiment Labs reads your Google data unless you ask us to, it’s needed for security, or the law requires it.

5. Who helps us run the service

We share information only with companies that help us run Experiment AI, and only what they need:

  • Google Firebase: sign-in and our database.
  • Vercel: hosting, the AI Gateway that connects to AI models, and Blob file storage.
  • AI model providers: the companies listed in section 3, for the content you send them, including dictation.
  • Brave Search: web searches.
  • Stripe: payments and billing.
  • Resend: verification and account emails.
  • Zoho: our email inbox, for messages you send us.
  • Google Analytics: cookie-free page statistics.
  • Google, Notion, GitHub and Canva: only if you connect them.

We may also share information if the law requires it, to protect people’s safety or our rights, or if Experiment Labs is involved in a merger or sale, in which case this policy will continue to apply to your information.

6. Cookies and browser storage

We don’t use cookies. Your browser does store a few things for the site to work: your sign-in (kept by Firebase), your app preferences (such as theme and text size) and a copy of your chats and projects for speed. Logging out clears the copy of your chats. You can clear the rest at any time in your browser settings.

7. How long we keep it

  • Account details, chats, projects, settings, Memory and connected services: until you delete them or your account.
  • Files and media: until you delete your account. Deleting your account deletes them from our storage.
  • Usage counts: today’s and this week’s, replaced as each day and week begins.
  • Verification codes: until they’re used, replaced or expire.
  • Payment records: Stripe keeps them as the law requires.
  • Server logs and analytics: for the periods our providers keep them.

8. Your choices and rights

In Settings › Account you can export everything stored about you or delete your account, which removes your chats, projects, Memory, files, connected services and usage, and cancels a paid plan. You can also email contact@experimentai.dev to see, correct, export or delete your information. Depending on where you live (for example, in the EU, the UK or California), you may have more rights, and we’ll honor them. We don’t sell or share personal information for cross-context advertising.

9. Security

Everything travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Your chats, Memory and connected-service access are kept on our servers where browsers can’t read them directly, verification codes are stored only in hashed form, and access to our systems is limited. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong password and tell us right away if you notice anything wrong.

10. Children

Experiment AI isn’t meant for children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect their information. If you believe a child has given us information, email us and we’ll delete it.

11. Where your data is processed

Our providers process data in the United States and other countries. Wherever it’s processed, we protect it as this policy describes.

12. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we’ll update the date at the top. If the change is significant, we’ll also let you know by email or in the app before it takes effect.

13. Contact

Questions about privacy? Email contact@experimentai.dev.
