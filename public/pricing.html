Every paid plan includes all five: Grok, Claude, Gemini, ChatGPT and Meta Muse. Free includes a limited selection.

What are side-by-side answers?

Ask once and get answers from two or more AIs next to each other, like Claude and ChatGPT. Compare them and keep the best. It’s on every plan, Free included.

Light gives you 5× the usage of Free. Pro doubles Light. Max 5× gives you five times Pro, and Max 10× gives you ten times Pro.
