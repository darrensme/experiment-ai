About us

We’re Experiment Labs, a small studio building simple, premium AI tools for everyone.

Experiment Labs started with a simple frustration: the best AI models live in different apps, each with its own subscription, its own limits and its own strengths. Switching between them gets old fast.

So we’re building Experiment AI: one place where Grok, Claude, Gemini, ChatGPT and Meta Muse work together. Pick any model, any time. Plan your week, connect your tools, make images and write code, all in one chat.

We sweat the details. Everything we make should feel simple to use and premium to hold, and we never stop experimenting to make it better. It’s right there in the name.
