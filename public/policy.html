Privacy policy

What we collect, why we collect it, and the choices you have.

Last updated October 30, 2026

Experiment Labs (“we”, “us”) makes Experiment AI. This policy explains what we collect when you use experimentai.dev and the Experiment AI app (together, the “service”), how we use it, who helps us run the service, and the choices you have.

The short version: we collect only what we need to run Experiment AI, we never sell your personal information, and we don’t use cookies.

1. What we collect

  • Account details. Your name and email address. If you sign up with email, you also choose a password, which is handled by Firebase Authentication; we never see it. If you sign in with Google, we receive the name, email address and profile photo on your Google account.
  • Waitlist details. Your place in line, how you signed up, and when you joined.
  • Email verification. When you sign up with email, we send you a 6-digit code. We store only a scrambled (hashed) version of the code, along with when it was sent and how many attempts were made, so we can stop abuse.
  • What you put into Experiment AI. Once the app is available: the messages you send, the files and folders you upload, the answers you get back, your chat history, and your Memory if it’s switched on.
  • Connected services. Only if you choose to connect them: Google Drive (only the files you pick), Google Calendar, Notion and GitHub. We access only what you allow, and you can disconnect at any time.
  • Usage information. We use Google Analytics in a cookie-free mode to count page views and see roughly where visitors come from and which devices they use. Our host, Vercel, keeps standard server logs (such as IP address, browser type and time of request) for security and reliability.
  • Messages to us. If you email contact@experimentai.dev, we keep that conversation.
  • Payments. When paid plans open, payments will be handled by our payment provider. We won’t store your full card number.

2. How we use it

  • To create your account, confirm your email and save your place on the waitlist.
  • To run Experiment AI: answer your messages, work with your files, remember things when Memory is on, and use the services you connect.
  • To email you about your account, your verification code and Experiment AI’s launch. We don’t send unrelated marketing.
  • To keep the service secure, prevent abuse and fix problems.
  • To understand which pages people use, so we can improve the site.
  • To follow the law.

We don’t sell your personal information, and we don’t use it to show you ads.

3. AI models and your content

Experiment AI gives you models from several companies, including Anthropic (Claude), OpenAI (ChatGPT), Google (Gemini), xAI (Grok), Meta (Muse) and Inception (Mercury). When you send a message, your message, any files you attach and the relevant part of the conversation go to the model you choose, through Vercel’s AI Gateway, so the model can answer. Each provider processes that content under its own terms for business use of its models.

  • We don’t train AI models on your content.
  • Research: when you switch Research on, Experiment AI may search the web to answer your question.
  • Memory: on paid plans, Memory is on by default so Experiment AI can remember useful details across chats. You can switch it off for any chat, and you can ask us to delete your saved memory at any time.

4. Google user data

If you connect Google Drive or Google Calendar, Experiment AI’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. In particular:

  • We use Google data only to provide the features you ask for, such as reading a file you picked or checking your calendar.
  • We don’t use Google data for advertising, and we don’t sell it.
  • We don’t use Google Workspace data to develop, improve or train generalized AI or machine learning models.
  • No person at Experiment Labs reads your Google data unless you ask us to, it’s needed for security, or the law requires it.

5. Who helps us run the service

We share information only with companies that help us run Experiment AI, and only what they need:

  • Google Firebase: sign-in and our database.
  • Google Analytics: cookie-free page statistics.
  • Vercel: hosting the website and app, and the AI Gateway that connects to AI models.
  • AI model providers: the companies listed in section 3, for the messages you send them.
  • Resend: sending verification and account emails.
  • Zoho: our email inbox, for messages you send us.
  • Our payment provider: once paid plans open.

We may also share information if the law requires it, to protect people’s safety or our rights, or if Experiment Labs is involved in a merger or sale, in which case this policy will continue to apply to your information.

6. Cookies and browser storage

We don’t use cookies. Your browser does store a few things for the site to work: your sign-in (kept by Firebase), whether you’ve dismissed the “no cookies” note, and app preferences such as whether the sidebar is hidden. You can clear these at any time in your browser settings.

7. How long we keep it

  • Account and waitlist details: until you delete your account.
  • Verification codes: until they’re used, replaced or expire.
  • Chats, files and Memory: until you delete them or your account.
  • Server logs and analytics: for the periods our providers keep them.

8. Your choices and rights

You can ask us to show you, correct, export or delete your personal information, or to delete your account, by emailing contact@experimentai.dev. Depending on where you live (for example, in the EU, the UK or California), you may have additional rights, and we’ll honor them. We don’t sell or share personal information for cross-context advertising.

9. Security

Everything travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS), verification codes are stored only in hashed form, and access to our systems is limited. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong password and tell us right away if you notice anything wrong.

10. Children

Experiment AI isn’t meant for children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect their information. If you believe a child has given us information, email us and we’ll delete it.

11. Where your data is processed

Our providers process data in the United States and other countries. Wherever it’s processed, we protect it as this policy describes.

12. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we’ll update the date at the top. If the change is significant, we’ll also let you know by email or in the app before it takes effect.

13. Contact

Questions about privacy? Email contact@experimentai.dev.
